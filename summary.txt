World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's US Senate race, beating Cindy Hyde-Smith by about 4,585 votes to become its first Black senator since Reconstruction and its first Democratic senator since 1982; Democrats hold 55 seats, Alaska uncalled (AP, Reuters).

Leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, where UN Secretary-General Guterres warned the slow response to climate change had put the world "on a path to destruction" (AFP).

Israeli forces killed at least seven people in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son, health officials said; Israel's new parliament was sworn in with no government yet after an election that cost Netanyahu his majority.

Saudi Arabia said it intercepted seven Houthi missiles and drones, including cruise missiles aimed at Riyadh.

British police arrested a US Army officer on suspicion of spying for Russia, and NATO scrambled jets after drones strayed into Baltic airspace amid Ukrainian strikes on Russia.

Ukraine said it struck Russia's Ust-Luga oil port, damaging five tanks, while Russian strikes hit six Ukrainian substations and a Chornomorsk market, killing three people (Ukrainian officials).
